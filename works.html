A data-driven reading of India’s economy from 2004 to 2026. Interactive charts and three original indices—Statistical Suppression, Fiscal Centralization and Democratic Quality—set beside the headline growth figures to ask how the numbers are made, and who decides what counts.

How India Lives

howindialives.impactmojo.in

Two hundred and five choropleth maps across 36 states and union territories and twelve themes—health, the economy, gender, education, religion, food and the environment—showing how much the national average hides, and why one policy rarely fits a country this varied.
